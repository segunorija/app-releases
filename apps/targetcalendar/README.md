# TargetCalendar

Track daily GBP targets and actuals in a month-view calendar. A tick marks a met target. Entries stay on your device, and Export to Excel lets you save or share a workbook containing your recorded months.

## Android downloads

TargetCalendar 1.1.0 supports Android 8.0 (API 26) and later. Look for the TargetCalendar release and download the `.apk` attachment. Each release has its own versioned tag and checksum.
